=== Finding: nginx keeps upstream connections open (keep-alive), so pf rule changes do not affect them
Observed on Mac 3, 23 Nov 2026 ~20:55, two requests through https://app.nova.test then netstat:
  1 s after:  tcp4  10.7.1.218.3001  10.7.5.201.56237  ESTABLISHED
  7 s after:  tcp4  10.7.1.218.3001  10.7.5.201.56237  TIME_WAIT   (Backend A / Node closed the idle connection after ~5 s)
Consequence: T3 run 1 (20:52:04) loaded the silent-drop rules while such a connection was open; pf is stateful and
only filters new connections, so requests 2 and 4 reused the open connection and were served by A in 0.04 s.
Fix for T3: after loading the fault rules, wait 10 s with no traffic so Node closes the idle connection; then time.
(nginx 1.31.6 uses HTTP/1.1 to upstreams by default since 1.29.7.)
